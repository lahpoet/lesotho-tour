A little about our country

Lesotho is a landlocked country in Southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. Its capital city is Maseru. The country is known for its high altitude, mountain scenery and cold winters.

Sesotho is the main local language, and English is also used. Basotho culture includes traditional music, clothing, crafts, food and stories passed between generations.

Things visitors can do

  • Visit mountain villages and scenic viewpoints.
  • Learn about Basotho history and traditions.
  • Visit craft markets and try local food.
  • Go hiking or horse riding with a local guide.

Travel tip

Weather in the mountains can change quickly. Bring warm clothes and ask local guides about road conditions.
